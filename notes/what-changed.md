# What changed since v1

> Required by the v2 brief: "what you changed since v1, and why." Filled in as
> v2 is built. Every entry points back to the v1 feedback (`notes/v2-plan.md`)
> or a decision in `notes/build-log.md`. Compare with v1: `git diff v1..main`.

| Change | Why (from v1) | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Landing page:** headline + three illustrated steps (Ideas → Development → Finished) instead of a paragraph | My cousin: *"took a while to read the intro page"* | #1 · `app/page.tsx`, `components/HowItWorks.tsx` |
| **"Saved to Ideas"** + one next step (**Add another idea**) right after saving an idea, shown once | My cousin: *"after they created an Idea they didn't know what to do next"* | #2 · `components/SavedBanner.tsx` |
| **Save and add another** on the idea form; **+ New idea** on the Ideas tab and idea pages | My cousin: *"didn't know how to create multiple ideas"* | #3 · `components/IdeaForm.tsx`, `app/d/[id]/page.tsx` |
| **? How it works** guide in the header, one-line tab hints, shorter copy everywhere: the main screens went from **300 → 149 words** (`npm run words`) | My cousin: *"text heavy… a lot of reading to understand what the different tabs are"*; wanted a read-me | #4 · `components/HelpButton.tsx`, `verify/words-v1.txt` vs `words-v2.txt` |
| **README: How to use it** in three steps | My cousin wanted *"a read me or instructions"* | #4 · `README.md` |
| **Tabs at the top of every page** (Ideas · Development · Recipes), always one tap away | My cousin found the tabs hard to understand; I wanted them reachable from anywhere, not only on the home page | `components/TopTabs.tsx` |
| **"Finished" renamed "Recipes"** (label only; the stored value stays `finished` because v1 shares the database) | "I don't like finished": *Recipes* is plainer (Ideas → Development → Recipes) | `lib/types.ts` |
| **Type or dictate** the cook's words, said everywhere; a new headline about easy recipe development; a landing page that fits on one screen at 5 sizes | Typing should be an obvious option; *"the text is too big. I want to minimize scrolling"* | `app/page.tsx`, `components/HowItWorks.tsx` |
| **One start button per tab**: + New idea / + Cook's words / New ▾; the empty lab book shows the three steps + that button | New, Save an idea, and Add the cook's words did the same thing on one screen (redundant) | `app/page.tsx` |
| **Quick-add box on the Ideas tab**: type an idea, press Enter, and it's in the list; the box clears for the next one | Creating an idea took a tab, a button, and a separate page; also answers my cousin's "how do I add more ideas" | `components/QuickAddIdea.tsx` |
| **Add a recipe by hand** (+ New recipe on Recipes): name, whose recipe, ingredients, numbered steps with optional "why it works", notes; saved in the same recipe format as the AI's, so editing, the science icon, Show sources, and send-by-link all work | Not every recipe needs the AI: some are already written down (a recipe card, a cookbook) | `components/ManualRecipeForm.tsx`, `createManualRecipe` |
| **Write the recipe yourself from Development**: a link under the AI panel (and in the no-words box) opens the recipe form prefilled with the item's name, cook, and notes; saving moves the item to Recipes | Writing a recipe by hand was only on the Recipes tab; an item already in Development needed a way that skips the AI | `app/d/[id]/CreateRecipe.tsx`, `app/recipes/new/page.tsx` |
| **Enter for the next line** in the recipe form: Enter adds the next ingredient or step right below and moves the cursor there; Shift+Enter is a new line inside a step | Typing a recipe in should be fast, without reaching for "+ Add" after every line | `components/ManualRecipeForm.tsx` |
| **Tags in the Recipes tab**: filter chips (All + your tags); on a recipe, a row of small chips ending in "+ Tag": tap it, type, press Enter (your tags appear as suggestions while typing, a new name makes a new tag, × removes it); an **All tags** page listing every tag with its recipes, where you add and delete tags (deleting keeps the recipes); a starter list (American, Indian, Mexican, Desserts) when you have none | My cousin asked for a way to categorize recipes (issue #8); a flan is both Mexican and a dessert. First built as "sections" with tap chips; renamed to tags with a type-to-add box because a recipe can have several and typing scales past a dozen tags | `app/tags/`, `components/TagPicker.tsx`, migration `20261121020000_sections.sql` |
| **Photos on ideas**: a camera button in the Ideas quick-add (text, a photo, or both; Enter saves); thumbnails in the lists; a Photos section on each idea and recipe to add more (up to 6), tap to view large in place (Previous/Next), or delete; thumbnails show the whole photo, never cropped | My cousin wanted more pictures; inspiration is often a photo of a dish, not words (issue #7) | `components/QuickAddIdea.tsx`, `components/EntryPhotos.tsx`, `lib/photos.ts`, migration `20261127000000_photos.sql` |
| **"Kitchen notes" instead of "the cook's words"**, everywhere (+ Kitchen notes, Add/Edit/Delete kitchen notes, the AI step, How it works), plus a tip wherever you add them: include amounts and as much detail as you can | The app is for developing recipes from rough instructions you dictate or paste, not only for recording someone else's words; more detail gives the AI less to ask about | `components/DictationEditor.tsx`, `app/new/`, `components/ui.tsx` (`KitchenNotesTip`) |
| **"Inspiration" instead of "Idea notes"** | With kitchen notes added, two kinds of "notes" on one page would be confusing | `components/InlineEdit.tsx`, `components/IdeaForm.tsx` |
| **Inspiration in the quick-add**: name, photo, and inspiration in one step | Before, you had to save the idea, open it, and tap the pencil to say what inspired it; I wanted fewer taps | `components/QuickAddIdea.tsx`, `quickAddIdea` |
| **Compact lists**: no "Move to" on every row (it's on the item's page); smaller names, one meta line, one preview line, smaller thumbnails (about half the height per row) | The repeated "Move to" under every item was noisy, and fewer rows fit on a phone screen | `app/page.tsx` |
| **Compact idea page**: smaller title with the date on the breadcrumb line, tighter sections, smaller photos with an add tile beside them, lighter AI panels, Move to and Delete idea on one line (about 30% shorter on a phone) | Less scrolling on a phone | `app/d/[id]/page.tsx`, `components/EntryPhotos.tsx` |
| **Compact recipe page**: smaller title and headings, tighter ingredient and step rows (step number on the first line), a lighter Send panel, short Edit/Delete under the kitchen notes, Move to and Delete recipe on one line (about 25% shorter on a phone) | Less scrolling while cooking from a phone | `components/RecipeView.tsx`, `app/r/[id]/page.tsx` |
| **Recipe page shows only the recipe**: inspiration, photos, kitchen notes, and the AI's questions fold into one closed "Notes from Ideas and Development" row | Too many different things on the page; once it's a recipe, the notes are history | `app/r/[id]/page.tsx` |
| **Dish photo on recipes**: beside the title and ingredients (add, tap to zoom, change, remove); it's the recipe's thumbnail in the list | A recipe should show what the dish looks like; inspiration photos are a different thing and stay in the notes (issue #7) | `components/DishPhoto.tsx`, migration `20261128000000_dish_photo.sql` |
| **Crop photos**: when adding a dish photo or later (Original, Square, Wide, Tall; drag and zoom) | Photos come in every shape; cropping lets the photo fit beside the ingredients with no gap | `components/PhotoCropper.tsx` (react-easy-crop, MIT) |
| **Ingredient notes behind icons; one "All notes" switch**: sliders for taste notes, a note icon otherwise, the flask for food science on steps; every note is muted (thin gray rule); "All notes" on the breadcrumb line opens or closes them all | The recipe was busy with notes everywhere; colored panels were too loud | `components/RecipeExtras.tsx`, `app/r/[id]/page.tsx` |
| **Compact Development page**: long kitchen notes show 6 lines with "Show all"; empty Inspiration and Photos take one line each; shorter headers (about 25% shorter on a phone) | The AI step was pushed far down by a long dictation | `components/ClampText.tsx`, `components/InlineEdit.tsx`, `components/EntryPhotos.tsx` |
| **Simpler tab headers and list rows**: the title is the tab's name ("Recipes"), with no count or description line; tags sit on the name's line in lists | Too many sizes and styles in one place; the tabs at the top already say where you are | `app/page.tsx` |
| **One font: Figtree** (headings in semibold), replacing Instrument Serif + Jost | The two fonts felt like they conflicted; I compared 14 options in the browser and chose one friendly, readable font | `app/layout.tsx`, `app/globals.css`, `notes/design/font-options-2.html` |
| **No "Show sources" switch or source labels** on recipes (still stored with every recipe) | I didn't need them, and the recipe page is calmer without them. Trade-off: AI suggestions no longer look different from the cook's own amounts | `components/RecipeExtras.tsx` |
| **Leave the AI questions**: Save for later (answers kept) or Discard questions (draft deleted, start over) | The questions page had no way out except finishing it | `saveAnswersForLater`, `discardDraft` |
| **Delete their words** (with a confirm), and **no original versions kept** (editing replaces, deleting removes everything) | I want to be able to delete the cook's words, and I don't need old versions | `deleteWords`, migrations `20261121*` |
